Works at the intersection of software, AI agents, and hardware engineering.

Sollemdev is an independent software and engineering studio operating across modular web architectures, AI productivity software, and pulse power electronics. Not from being scattered, but because the boxes were never big enough — one rigorous mind applied to different materials.

Everything starts from first principles. Nothing gets taken at “it just works” — there's always a dig down to the actual physics or logic underneath. Slower to start, relentless once the foundation clicks. Modular web systems with editorial discipline. AI-agent orchestration and software products that do real work. High-voltage pulse electronics where the gap defines whether the pulse shapes right. Licensed software products, not weekend toys. Same structural rigor underneath.

The core engineering philosophy is simple: designing a high-performance user interface, an AI translation pipeline, and a pulse-forming network are the same discipline — structure, precision, and knowing exactly what to remove.
