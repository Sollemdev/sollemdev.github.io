Refund & Cancellation Policy

Terms Privacy Refunds ← Back to Home
Last updated: October 4, 2026 · Terms of Service · Privacy Policy · ← Sollemdev Studio

1. Overview

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains how refunds are handled for voluntary donations, subscriptions, and digital products purchased through sollemdev.github.io by Sollemdev, an independent software laboratory operated by a self-employed individual residing in Ukraine.

Because we sell through two channels, the party responsible for your refund depends on where you bought the product. Please check section 3 before submitting a request.

This Policy is intended for users aged 18 or older, as set out in our Terms of Service. Billing support: sollemdev@gmail.com.

2. Voluntary Donations

  • Nature of donations: Contributions made through the Lava.top donation widget are voluntary, gratuitous gifts supporting independent research, tools, and server costs.
  • General rule: Because a donation is not a purchase of goods or services, it is generally not refundable once you have intentionally completed it.
  • Erroneous or duplicate transactions: If an unintended charge occurs — a duplicate billing, a technical failure, or an unauthorised transaction — you are entitled to a full refund.
  • Request window: Refund requests for erroneous donations must be submitted within 14 calendar days of the transaction date.

3. Digital Products: Who Handles Your Refund

Digital products are sold through two independent channels with different sellers of record:

  • Babel Pro subscriptions and boost packs purchased on Lava.top — seller: Sollemdev (the Operator). Lava.top acts only as a payment agent collecting funds on our behalf. Refund requests are reviewed and issued by Sollemdev under this Policy. Because we are the seller, applicable taxes on these transactions are our responsibility, and we can issue an invoice in our name on request.
  • Boost packs purchased on our Gumroad storefront — seller: Gumroad, Inc. Gumroad acts as the merchant of record and resells the product to you. Refunds, chargebacks, and buyer disputes for these purchases are handled by Gumroad under the Gumroad Terms of Service and Gumroad’s own refund rules, and Gumroad is responsible for applicable indirect taxes. You can also contact us and we will help you route the request.

If you are unsure which channel you used, check the receipt: it names the seller.

4. Subscriptions & Boost Packs

  • 14-day money-back guarantee: For a Babel Pro subscription purchased from Sollemdev, or the first activation of a paid tier, you may request a 100% refund within 14 calendar days of payment if the software did not suit your needs or you encountered an unresolvable technical defect.
  • Subscription cancellation: You may cancel recurring billing at any time through your customer portal on Lava.top (or Gumroad, for purchases made there). Cancellation stops future charges. Access continues until the end of the period already paid; partial refunds for unused days of the current period are not provided.
  • Boost packs: On-demand boost packs are indivisible digital goods with immediate effect. A refund may be requested within 14 days only if the pack has not been consumed.
  • Your statutory rights: Where mandatory consumer law grants you stronger rights — for example, a statutory withdrawal period in your country of residence — those rights prevail over this Policy.

5. How to Request a Refund

Send an email to sollemdev@gmail.com with the subject line Refund Request — [Order ID], including:

  1. Date and time of payment;
  2. Amount and currency;
  3. The channel used (Lava.top or Gumroad);
  4. Transaction or Order ID from your receipt;
  5. Reason for the request;
  6. The email address used at checkout.

If the purchase was made on Gumroad, we will forward your request or direct you to the Gumroad support flow, since Gumroad is the seller for that transaction.

6. Processing Timelines

  • Requests relating to purchases where Sollemdev is the seller are reviewed within 3 business days.
  • Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method.
  • Funds typically appear on your statement within 3 to 10 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer.
  • Requests for Gumroad purchases follow Gumroad’s own review and payout timelines.

7. Chargebacks

If you believe a charge is incorrect, please contact us before filing a chargeback with your bank. We resolve verified erroneous charges quickly, and contacting us first usually produces a faster outcome than a dispute procedure.

8. Governing Law

This Policy is governed by the laws of Ukraine. Nothing in it limits any mandatory consumer protection rights available to you under the law of your own country of residence.
