1. Data Controller & Legal Basis

This Privacy Policy explains how Sollemdev (the “Operator”, “we”, “us”), an independent software laboratory operated by a natural person acting as a self-employed individual residing in Ukraine, processes personal data when you use sollemdev.github.io, install the Babel browser extension, make a donation, or contact us.

We process personal data in accordance with applicable data protection law, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where it applies. Our lawful bases are the performance of a contract (processing your order or licence), our legitimate interest in operating and securing our services, your consent (where you contact us or accept analytics), and compliance with legal obligations.

2. Data Minimization & Local-First Architecture

Our guiding principle is data minimization. We collect only what is necessary to answer your messages, deliver the software you licensed, and keep the service secure. We never sell, rent, or monetize personal data.

3. Data We Process

  • Contact forms and direct email: name or nickname, email address, topic, and the body of your message. Provided voluntarily so that we can reply.
  • Donations and purchases (Lava.top / Gumroad): payment details such as card numbers, billing data, and bank details are handled directly by the payment providers on their PCI DSS certified infrastructure. Sollemdev never receives, stores, or views your card numbers. We receive only a transaction confirmation such as an order identifier, amount, and date.
  • Local browser storage and analytics: your theme choice is stored locally in your browser via localStorage — it is not transmitted to us. We do not set first-party cookies, and no language preference is persisted. Anonymised traffic statistics are collected by Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-894W8CYHGQ), which may set its own cookies subject to your browser and consent settings.
  • Babel browser extension:
    • Settings such as hotkeys, target language, and operation mode are stored locally in chrome.storage.local.
    • Text you submit for translation or prompt refinement is transmitted only at the moment you press the hotkey, and is passed to the AI model solely to generate the result. Your text is not stored in databases, not archived, and not used for model training.
    • Client-side Data Loss Prevention runs entirely on your device and blocks or masks passwords, API keys, and card numbers before any request leaves the browser.

4. Purposes of Processing

  • Performing our obligations under the Terms of Service and delivering licensed software;
  • Providing technical support and responding to enquiries;
  • Recording donations, purchases, and processing refund requests for erroneous transactions;
  • Protecting the infrastructure and preventing abuse.

5. Third-Party Providers

We share personal data only with the providers required to run the service:

  • Payments: Lava.top (donations, subscriptions, and boost packs sold by the Operator) and Gumroad, Inc. (boost packs sold by Gumroad as merchant of record);
  • Hosting: GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages) for the static website;
  • Fonts: Google LLC (Google Fonts) for typeface delivery;
  • Analytics: Google LLC (Google Analytics 4) for anonymised visit statistics;
  • AI inference providers: third-party model APIs that process text transiently to return a translation or refined prompt.

6. International Transfers

Some of these providers are located outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area. Where personal data is transferred internationally, we rely on the safeguards offered by those providers, such as standard contractual clauses and adequacy decisions.

7. Retention & Security

Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed to resolve your enquiry. Transaction records are retained as long as required for accounting and tax purposes. All website traffic is transmitted over HTTPS (TLS). We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, or loss.

8. Age Requirement

Our website, software, and paid offerings are intended for users aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a minor has provided us with personal data, contact sollemdev@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

9. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to your personal data, ask for rectification of inaccurate data, request erasure, restrict or object to processing, and request data portability. You may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

Local extension data can be cleared at any time using the “Reset stats” control in the extension settings, or by uninstalling the extension.

To exercise any of these rights, write to sollemdev@gmail.com. Requests are answered within 10 business days.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The current version is always available at https://sollemdev.github.io/projects/privacy.html.
